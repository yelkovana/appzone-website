Privacy Policy and Legal Disclaimer

1. General Principles

At Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi, protecting user data and ensuring privacy are among our core values.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data and clarifies our legal and intellectual property framework for this application.

The App currently does not collect, store, or process any personal data. No registration, login, or tracking of identifiable information is performed.

All user interactions remain on the device and are not transmitted to any server owned or controlled by Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi.

2. Use of User Information

Since no personal data is collected or processed by the App, no user information is stored, analyzed, or used for marketing, advertising, or analytics purposes.

The App does not contact users or process any background communication.

3. Data Access and Sharing

The App does not access, record, or share any user content, device data, or identifiable information.

No user data is transferred to Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi or to third parties.

Any data processed through third-party AI services (listed below) occurs directly between the user and the third-party provider, under their own privacy terms.

4. Technical Data and IP Information

The App does not log or record IP addresses, cookies, device identifiers, or technical metadata.

No analytics SDKs or tracking tools are implemented.

5. Categories of Data Collected

No personal or anonymous data is collected, stored, or transmitted by Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi.

If third-party AI tools collect information (e.g., API usage logs), such data remains under the control of those third parties and is subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Purposes of Data Usage

Since no data is collected, there are no purposes for data processing or analytics.

7. Data Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not share any data with third parties.

However, users may voluntarily use external AI generation services through the App interface. These services include:

Sora
Veo
Kling AI
Fal AI

All AI processing (video, image, or media generation) occurs outside the App, through these providers' APIs.

8. Legal Exceptions

Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi does not collect user data, so no disclosures to authorities or third parties occur.

If users directly interact with external providers (e.g., Sora, Veo, Kling, Fal AI), those providers' legal terms apply independently.

9. User Rights and Choices

Since no personal data is collected or processed, users do not need to take any action regarding data access or deletion.

If you have any questions or requests, you may contact us at: help@appzonedev.com

10. Data Security and Confidentiality

Although no personal data is collected or transmitted, Appzone Dijital Hizmetler Anonim Şirketi implements best practices to ensure that the App operates securely and without unauthorized data flow.

Users are encouraged to use official app stores and avoid sharing sensitive data within third-party integrations.

13. Data Collection

Data Collection for AI Analysis: Our AI Creator app allows users to upload videos to analyze their viral potential. When you explicitly consent and select a video, it is securely transmitted to our third-party AI service provider, Google (Google Gemini API). This data is strictly used for generating your video analysis report and is not utilized for any other purposes. Google provides the same or equal protection of user data as outlined in our privacy policy.

AI Analysis and Third-Party Processing: To provide our video analysis features, the video content you choose to analyze may be securely transmitted to a third-party artificial intelligence service provider, such as Google (Google Gemini API). This processing is necessary to generate insights, analytics, and reports about the potential performance of your video content.

Your video is shared with the third-party provider only after you explicitly grant permission within the application. The content is used solely for the purpose of generating the requested analysis and is not used for any other purpose by our service.

We take reasonable measures to ensure that any third-party providers we use process data in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection standards. However, once the content is transmitted to the third-party provider, it will be subject to that provider's own privacy policies and terms.

By allowing the analysis feature, you consent to the secure transfer and processing of your selected video by the third-party AI provider for the limited purpose described above.
